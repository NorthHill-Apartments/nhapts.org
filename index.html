Qualifications for Residency

Residency at any of our units must meet income eligibility requirements. In addition, the following apply:

  • An non-refundable application fee of $30.00 per person or $40.00 per married couple must accompany the application.
  • Upon acceptance, a security deposit must be paid with the first month's rent when the lease is signed.
  • No more than two unrelated parties may occupy an apartment and no more than 2 persons per bedroom.
  • At no time can all occupants of a home be full-time students without meeting specific qualifications.
  • Income limits apply only at the time of of the original lease.
  • Recertification must be done each year; however, there are no income limits.
  • Total household income of all residents must be stated and verified to meet Section 42 eligibility guidelines.
Specific details can be discussed at time of application.
